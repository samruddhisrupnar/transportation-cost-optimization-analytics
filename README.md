# Cost Optimization in Goods Transportation Using Analytics

##  Project Overview
This repository contains the Business Analysis and Data Analytics project conducted for **Sairaja Roadways**. The primary objective of this initiative was to evaluate end-to-end transportation operations, identify performance gaps, analyze major cost drivers, and deliver data-driven insights using **ServiceNow Reporting** and **Advanced Excel** to optimize logistics expenses and improve overall business outcomes.

##  Tech Stack & Tools Used
* **Enterprise Platform & Analytics:** ServiceNow (Reporting Module, Performance Analytics, Dashboards)
* **Data Manipulation & Triage:** Advanced Excel (VLOOKUP/XLOOKUP, Pivot Tables)
* **Business Analysis:** Process Mapping, Gap Analysis, Operational KPI Frameworks
* **Documentation:** Markdown

##  Key Responsibilities & Workflows

### 1. Business Process & Gap Analysis
* Analyzed end-to-end transportation workflows to understand core logistics needs and operational requirements.
* Conducted gap analysis to pinpoint transportation bottlenecks and operational inefficiencies.

### 2. Data Engineering & Governance
* Managed, extracted, and validated operational data natively within **ServiceNow** and **Advanced Excel**.
* Maintained data integrity across tickets and fulfillment records to ensure reliable operational reporting.

### 3. Financial & Operational Analytics
* Performed comprehensive KPI, trend, and variance analysis to track key logistics performance measures.
* Deep-dived into operational datasets—specifically targeting **fuel consumption, toll fees, maintenance cycles, and delivery timelines**—to uncover underlying cost drivers.

### 4. ServiceNow Dashboards & Visual Reports
* Configured and customized **ServiceNow reports and interactive dashboards** to dynamically visualize operational trends and Key Performance Indicators (KPIs).
* Created visual charts within the platform to track incident counts, resolution times, and transport asset utilization.

### 5. Stakeholder Communication
* Synthesized platform analytics into strategic, data-driven recommendations.
* Presented findings using exported reports and dashboard screenshots to facilitate informed executive decision-making.
